01

Context

George Business is the business banking platform of Erste Group, built by George Labs for small and medium enterprises. It covers the essentials of everyday company banking: payments, managing accounts and cards, user management, currency exchange and much more.

02

Strategic problem

Erste Group operates in 7 countries, and each country had its own business banking platform. Every platform had a different look, structure and logic, so each of the 7 front-ends had to be designed, developed and maintained separately.

3 of 7 country platforms · all different

The challenge was to bring them together into one consistent experience that could scale across countries, languages and regulations. We unified the front-end while giving each country the flexibility to keep its own backend logic.

The platform also had to scale with each company's needs. Some companies have just a few accounts, others hundreds, with complex user management, many employees and accountant access. On top of that, very strict accessibility regulations applied. They didn't define the design, but they influenced many decisions and had to be taken into account throughout.
