01

Context

Ratings and key figures give companies an honest assessment of where they stand and valuable pointers for their next financial decisions. The Financial Health Zone is open to all companies that keep accounts, with an annual turnover between 1 and 50 million euros and financing with Erste Bank and Sparkasse.

02

Strategic problem

Business owners rarely know how their company compares with others in the same industry, or which financial figures deserve their attention.

Financial Health started in 2022 with 1,000 clients in Erste Bank Croatia. The challenge was to attract clients to the Zone and keep adding modules they find relevant.

04

Constraints and challenges

Each market uses it differently. In Czechia, technical stability, access permissions and interactivity needed work. In Romania, clients engage most when Financial Health is a decision-support tool linked to business outcomes.

05

Outcome and impact

Comparison: shows where the company stands against its industry.

Overview: explains which financial figures to keep an eye on.

Financial Health is a core pillar of market differentiation and of the client engagement model, supported by peer comparison, transparency and advisory conversations. Long-term value sits where actionable insights, relationship-manager-led advisory and scalable digital capabilities meet.
